Neighborhood bookshop, est. 2011

Shelves stocked by people who actually read the books.

Fernwood Books is a small, independent shop on the corner of Elm and 9th. We hand-pick every title, host a monthly reading circle, and keep the lights on late for anyone who needs one more chapter before closing.
