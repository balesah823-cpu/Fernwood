About Fernwood Books

A shop built around slow reading and long conversations.

Fernwood Books opened in 2011 in a narrow storefront that used to be a shoe repair shop — you can still see the old counter marks in the wood floor. What started as two shelves of used paperbacks has grown into a full shop, but the goal hasn't changed: put good books in front of people who might not have found them otherwise.

We don't chase bestseller lists. Every title on our shelves has been read by someone on staff, and most of our recommendations come from actual conversations at the counter, not algorithms. If you tell us you loved a book, we can usually name three more you haven't heard of yet.

What we care about

Real recommendations

Staff picks are written by hand and updated every month, based on what we're actually reading and loving.

Local first

Over a third of our shelf space goes to authors and small presses from within a hundred miles of the shop.

No rush

Chairs are always free, the tea kettle is always on, and nobody will ever ask you to buy something to sit down.
